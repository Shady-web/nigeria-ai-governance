# Nigeria AI Governance Gap Analysis

**Researcher:** Siminloluwa 
**Status:** In Progress — Week 1 Complete  
**Target Output:** Academic research paper + policy brief  
**Strategic Purpose:** GIST AI Policy and Strategy M.S. Application, Spring 2027

## Research Question
Nigeria lacks a coherent AI governance framework while AI systems 
are already deployed across healthcare, finance, education, and 
public administration. This research maps that gap, examines why 
it exists, and proposes a practical framework Nigeria can adopt 
drawing from global best practices.
